# Wen Jing Ch.169-170 Lore & Visual Reference (zhanlong-v1)

## 1. Pre-Context (Ch.163-169)
- **Three-Void Entanglement (`三方虚空`)**: Beneath Xiyu Lake (`洗玉湖`), True World (`真界`), Shaman God's Water World (`水世界`), and fallen Shangqing HQ Taixiao Divine Court (`太霄神庭`, outer `四方八天` pried open by You Can) collide. Locked at Longevity True Person (`长生真人`), Yu Ci invoked `万古云霄`, draping his Inner Void (`心内虚空`) around the Court's core.
- **Xue Pingzhi's Chest/Back Blood (`"刚刚多谢了"`)**: She stood motionless behind Yu Ci with her `两仪圈` jade bangle balancing his Yin-Yang qi, taking Zhu Yang's invisible `天遁杀剑` through her own shoulder/chest/ribs rather than dodging.
- **Prior Borrowing of `掌刑神将` & `斩龙台`**: Minutes earlier in `玄明恭庆天` (Ch.166), Yu Ci dropped a `云楼树` leaf to project two 1.6-zhang `掌刑神将` and the phantom `斩龙台` (`锁龙柱/困龙桩`, `断龙石`, mutilated dragon phantoms).
- **`承启天` & `人间界`**: Outer layer `人间界` mirrors Xiyu Lake sky/water where You Huang (`幽煌`, turned by Mahakala `大黑天佛母菩萨` into an inward-coiling blazing sun-wheel `大日`) rampages. One tier inward lies `承启天` with `云楼树`, whose purple-qi branch pulls Yu Ci into the `万古云霄` Dao-Realm (`道境`).
- **Millennium-Old Shangqing Calamity**: Last Ziwei Emperor Yang Duanming (`杨端明`) practiced forbidden heart-demon arts, was corrupted by Demon Overlord Canluolina (`参罗利那`) + 10+ arch-demons, sabotaged the Four Emperors array from within, and crashed Taixiao Divine Court into Xiyu Lake. Four abroad Earth Immortals returned to a trap: two fell; the third, Qingjing Sanren (`清净散人`), destroyed the return beacon (`虚空标识`), placed the two fallen corpses into `勾陈` and `后土` seats to sustain self-purification, and burned his `清净法身` trapping Canluolina's avatar for 200+ years until co-founder Yang Hezi (`朝阳先生杨和子`) tore through space, beheaded Yang Duanming on `斩龙台`, sealed Canluolina + 10+ arch-demons inside his own torso with 20+ blood-rune chains, and froze `封神台` in the River of Time for 1,000 years.

## 2. Character & Prop Visual Bible
- **余慈 (`Yu Ci`)**: Slender young Taoist in spotless pale-jade wide-sleeved Taoist robe (`玉色道袍，大袖飘飘`), topknot; holds `赤金符诏` (fades to translucent lake-jade cyan `青光`); manifests bowl-sized cyan lotus (`碗大青莲`, dewdrops on petals).
- **杨和子 (`Yang Hezi`, 朝阳先生)**:
  1. *Disguised on Terrace*: Cliff-edge, wind-whipped Taoist robe, hands behind back; altered pale face, long beard stiff with dried clotted blood; hands blackened with coagulated blood; eyes flash crimson over endlessly layered abyssal green (`赤芒闪动，眼底幽绿层层叠叠`); faint water-mist cyan halo (`水烟清光`) atop crown; self-punch reveals **20+ wrist-thick blood-cinnabar Shangqing talisman chains piercing chest & abdomen**.
  2. *Chronal Rewind True Form*: Fighting swarms of demon shadows in blazing water-mist halo, left hand gripping weeping/snarling corrupted descendant **Yang Duanming (`杨端明`)**, right hand drawing blood-chains through his own chest, throwing Yang Duanming onto `斩龙台` to behead him; clears terrace, reveals **clear refined true face (`清朗面目`)**, fails to summon blade on himself, alters face walking to cliff edge.
  3. *Reborn Protocol-Spirit `"杨祖"`*: Ink-black demon blood vaporizes; jade-emerald blood (`碧血莹然如玉`) spurts, absorbed by cyan lotus on neck stump (`颈种莲花`); corpse sits up, stands straight, lotus light washes down forming Yang Hezi's true head—courteous, smiling, billowing sleeves, yet utterly devoid of mortal emotion (`依循道礼法度，再非血肉之情`). Flicks emerald blood into a misty rainbow bridge (`碧血化虹`).
- **薛平治 (`Xue Pingzhi`, 平治元君)**: Courtly robes (`华美裙服`), `两仪圈` jade bangle at wrist, dark crimson bloodstains on chest/back, gazing wistfully at Dao Palace flying eaves.
- **幽蕊 / 慕容轻烟 / 夏夫人**: Stationed at cloud nodes outside Dao Palace channeling surging spirit-veins.
- **承启天众人**: 赵相山 (plump scholar robe, futou cap, salute & telepathic commentary), 血府老祖 (grey-ink robe, salute), 虚生 (old Taoist collapsed drunk on pure qi `"醉灵"`), 影鬼 (flickering translucent dark shadow resembling Qu Wujie, waves hand).
- **青衣白鹤童子 & 两位金甲掌刑神将 & 虎辇玉舆隐轮之车**: Cyan-robed boy on white crane (`老爷，请随我来`, dissolves into smoke before terrace); two imposing golden-armored generals in helmets & cloaks (`主御`/`副御`, later flank `断龙石` shouting `"符诏至，刀来！"` ); wheel-less talisman-etched jade chariot pulled by roaring white tigers.
- **四御门庭**: Mottled, chipped, blood-stained nine-bay ten-pillar cloud archway (`九间十柱牌坊`); swarming black demon shadows melt like snow in boiling water under golden tadpole-script light (`沸汤沃雪`).
- **参罗利那本命分身**: 17-legged crimson-smoke spider phantom (`形如蜘蛛，十七长足，诡谲凶陋魔睛`) bursting from neck stump -> cleaved in two -> caught by sky-wide cyan edict net -> pelted by Dao-Realm immortals' thunderbolts -> sealed into talismanic silkworm cocoon (`如蚕茧一般`) -> cast into `万魔池`.
- **中天紫微星空大殿 & 清净散人**: Vast dark star-altar-palace; central spherical `三垣四象` cosmic embryo orbited by scattered star-rivers; behind it floats **Qingjing Sanren's half grey-black skeleton (`半具灰黑残缺骸骨`)**; shifting unfixed seats of `玉皇`, `勾陈`, `后土` (weighed down by two Earth Immortal corpses).
