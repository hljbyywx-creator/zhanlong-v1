Scene: the tranquil inner sanctum of Chengqi Heaven (承启天) — a wide, weathered ancient stone terrace suspended amid calm seas of pale xuan-paper mist. Rising from the rear-left of the stone platform is the colossal primordial Cloud-Tower Tree (云楼树), its gnarled trunk painted in bold axe-cut and dry-brush ink strokes, its vast overarching canopy spreading across the upper sky and trailing long, graceful, translucent curtains of pale violet-purple mineral wash and misty ink tendrils (淡紫墨韵霞气璎珞). The broad flagstone foreground-midground on the right is left spacious for composited character sprites, while the bottom fifth fades into calm mist and blank paper for subtitles. No people.
Colour: Southern Song ink-wash greys and rich black bark strokes, soft absorbed mineral violet-purple (藤紫淡染) and a whisper of pale gold dust in the hanging canopy mist, muted ochre in the ancient stone terrace.
