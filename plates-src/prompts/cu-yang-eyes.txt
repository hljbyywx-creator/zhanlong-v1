Scene: an arresting, monumental horizontal closeup of an ancient Taoist elder's weathered brow-bones and half-opened eyes (沧桑眉骨下似瞌非瞌的双眸骤睁) emerging across the upper-middle third of the frame out of rough dry-brush charcoal shadow and swirling aged xuan-paper mist. Drawn purely with bold Southern Song calligraphic brush strokes, axe-cut temple creases, and broken ink washes: beneath heavy, wind-worn ink brows, the archaic eyes part open — at the dead centre of each dark ink pupil burns a tiny, sharp pinpoint of flat mineral cinnabar-red (瞳仁一点朱砂赤芒), while inside the shadowed iris around it sink concentric, overlapping rings of deep malachite and dark moss-green mineral wash (眼底层层叠叠深不见底的石绿幽渊). Lower nose and face dissolve completely into pale xuan-paper mist; keep the bottom fifth completely calm empty mist for subtitles.
Colour: charcoal and iron-grey ink strokes on textured warm xuan paper, burnt ochre skin-shadow wash, concentrated mineral cinnabar-vermilion dot at pupil centre, layered deep malachite-green and dark moss washes in the iris depths.
