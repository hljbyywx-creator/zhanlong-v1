Scene: a high, melancholic aerial ink-wash vista looking down into boundless seas of drifting white and cool-grey clouds, through which the vast, half-ruined central palace precincts of the fallen Taixiao Divine Court (破败神庭中枢殿宇群) glimmer faintly far below — collapsed colonnades, silent courtyards, and tilted eaves emerging like ghostly ink memories in the deep mist.
