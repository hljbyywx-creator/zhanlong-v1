Style/medium: a Chinese ink-wash painting on warm off-white xuan rice paper, in the lineage of Southern Song Academy painting (Ma Yuan, Xia Gui, Liang Kai) — one consistent hand for the whole film. Visible paper fibres and grain everywhere, even inside the darkest ink; ink bleeds and feathers into the paper (洇).
Mood: cold, restrained, watchful — a lake under pale daylight where something deadly waits beneath the calm. The ink is mostly cool greys laid in thin wet washes over wide bare paper; then, only where force or danger is, sudden dense wet black ink, a few large axe-cut side-brush strokes (斧劈皴) and dry-brush flying-white (飞白) streaks. Strong value contrast at the focal point only; the rest stays quiet and airy. Every form is made of a FEW confident brush strokes, as if painted in minutes by a master; no fine detail spread evenly everywhere, no smooth airbrushed gradients.
Empty space (留白): at least a third of the picture is plain paper, pale wash, mist or open water.
Water, mist, smoke, light-waves and fire: large wet washes with bleeding edges plus a few long, fluid brush lines; water is suggested by a few horizontal dry strokes and blank paper, never by drawn ripple patterns; NO repeating scalloped or bubble-shaped cloud puffs, no fluffy photographic cumulus, no particles or sparks rendered as glowing dots.
Figures (only if the scene asks for them): freehand abbreviated-brush figures (写意人物) — a robe is a few broad wet strokes with flying-white at sleeves and hem, a head is the hair mass and hair knot; NEVER draw facial features (figures are seen from behind, side-back with the face turned away, in deep shadow, or as small silhouettes). Anatomy simple but correct: two arms, two legs, five fingers if a hand is visible at all.
Colour: ink first. Only the tints named in the scene description are allowed, applied like mineral pigment washes soaked into the paper (cinnabar 朱砂, rouge, azurite blue 石青, malachite green 石绿, ochre, pale gold), flat and absorbed, never glowing digital light.
Absolutely none of: game concept art, matte painting, thick digital oil or impasto painting (厚涂), 3D render, photorealism, photographic fire, water or glow, lens flare, bloom, glowing VFX; anime, manga or webtoon style, pretty idol faces; gore, wounds, blood splatter; text, calligraphy, legible characters, inscriptions, seals, red stamps, signatures, watermarks; borders, frames, scroll mounts, vignettes.
Composition: keep the bottom fifth of the picture free of key subjects (subtitle band).
Output: one full-bleed 16:9 landscape image, as large as possible (at least 1920×1080).
