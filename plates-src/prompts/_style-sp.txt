Style/medium: a single freehand ink FIGURE (写意人物) in the manner of Southern Song Academy figure painting and Liang Kai's abbreviated brush (梁楷减笔), painted as a CUT-OUT LAYER for an animation — the same hand and the same person as the attached reference figure. The figure is built from a few broad, confident strokes of varied ink — dense wet black, mid greys and pale washes laid in one go (浓淡墨) — with broken ink (破墨) where wet strokes run into each other; robe folds are only a few long strokes; the edges of sleeves, hems and hair show dry-brush flying-white (飞白) and split, feathered ends. It must look like a handsome, well-proportioned standing portrait (立绘) with a strong silhouette, NOT a flat uniform silhouette or paper cut-out, and NOT a modern illustration: no pretty idol face, no anime, no digital rendering, no airbrushed gradients, no fine fabric texture.
Faces: never draw facial features — the figure is seen from the back-side or in profile with the face turned away; a head is the hair mass, the hair knot and at most one thin contour line of cheek or jaw.
Background (critical): pure flat white #FFFFFF everywhere outside the figure — no paper tone, no paper grain, no texture, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no scenery, no vignette — so the figure can be cut out with a clean edge. Inside the figure keep the full range of ink tones.
Colour: ink only, black and neutral greys, except for any single colour accent the scene description explicitly names. Absolutely none of: text, calligraphy, legible characters, seals, red stamps, signatures; borders, frames; other people; glow or light effects.
Output: one full-bleed 16:9 landscape image, at least 1920×1080, the figure centred horizontally unless the scene description says otherwise.
