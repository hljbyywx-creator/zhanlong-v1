Scene: high amid the swirling gold-lined clouds and distant malachite mountain tips of the Taixiao Dao-Realm, a tempest of celestial Daoist judgment unfolds: dozens of jagged, calligraphic strokes of azurite-cyan and pale mud-gold lightning (万千金青雷霆) crash downward from every corner of the upper clouds onto a suspended central mass — an eerie, contorted dark charcoal-and-cinnabar shadow-form being tightly woven and sealed inside layers upon layers of luminous cyan-jade and fine gold silk-threads into a colossal suspended silkworm cocoon (符文金丝层层缠裹如巨茧). Wide silk-toned cloud bands frame the action; keep the bottom fifth calm pale cloud-wash free of busy detail for subtitles. No human figures.
Colour: crisp ink brush strokes on aged silk-toned paper, malachite green and azurite blue washes, fine pale mineral gold lightning and cocoon threads, dense charcoal-black with a hint of dark cinnabar trapped inside the cocoon core.
