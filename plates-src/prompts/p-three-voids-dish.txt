Scene: an awe-inspiring, abstract-monumental high-altitude Taoist cosmological vision (三方虚空·倒扣玉碟法象) painted in pure Southern Song xuan-paper ink and mineral wash: suspended amidst a boundless sea of dark grey storm-ink clouds is a colossal, dome-like inverted pale white-jade dish (倒扣温润白玉巨碟) spanning the middle of the frame, suggested by sweeping translucent jade-white washes and calligraphic contour strokes. Cradled beneath its central arch pools a vast, deep reservoir of malachite-emerald water (石绿碧水). Along roughly one-third of its outer rim on the right, the pale jade shell is fractured and breached, and cascading torrents of emerald-green water spill outward into the misty void below. Keep the bottom fifth quiet pale cloud-wash for subtitles. No figures.
Colour: charcoal and cool grey ink storm-clouds, warm translucent jade-white paper reserve, absorbed malachite green and pale azurite mineral water washes, subtle ochre mist.
