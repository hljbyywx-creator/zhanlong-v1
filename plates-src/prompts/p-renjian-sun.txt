Scene: mid-sky above Xiyu Lake in the turbulent outer mortal-mirror layer (人间界) — vast churning banks of wet-wash ink clouds and wind-lashed lake-water mist below. High in the distant upper-left sky, a ferocious inward-coiling solar wheel of dense flying-white ink and scorched mineral cinnabar-gold wash (狂暴赤金墨晕大日) churns the surrounding clouds into spiral shockwaves. Contrastingly, in the right-midground, a quiet wet-ink mountain ridge with ancient weathered stone steps winds peacefully inward toward a serene, mist-veiled inner sanctum.
